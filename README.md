# BlinkIT Grocery Sales Analysis
**Sapphire IQ – Retail & Grocery Sales Analytics** | Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Jupyter

## Objectives
1. Understand the business and its sales performance
2. Make sure the available information is reliable (data cleaning)
3. Find out what is driving better sales performance
4. Help management make better business decisions

## Key Insights
- Total sales: 1,201,681 across 1,559 products and 10 outlets; average rating 3.97/5
- Sales depend on how many outlets stock a product, not on visibility, weight or rating
- Grocery Stores sell ~40% less than Supermarkets because they stock ~40% fewer products
- 9 of 16 categories generate 80% of sales
